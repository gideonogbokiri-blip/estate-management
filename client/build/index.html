Estate Management
How the application works

From an empty estate to a running community.

This platform takes an estate from its first application through approval, setup and daily operations — with a separate, permission-scoped workspace for every person involved.

The journey, step by step

Every stage writes to the same estate database. Nothing is a mock screen once an estate is approved — records, decisions and history are persisted and audited.

1

Register the estate

An applicant creates an account and submits an estate application: name, address, homes, phases and the proposed management entity. Supporting documents can be attached; they are optional.

Applicant
2

Platform review

A platform reviewer checks the application and documents, assigns the review and chooses an eligible active account as the estate's first administrator. Approval creates the estate and membership together.

Platform admin
3

Set up operations

The estate administrator registers properties and owners, adds structure, invites staff, configures charges, facilities, gates and budgets, and publishes the first resident notice.

Estate manager
4

Residents and owners join

Residents request access to a property; landlords are verified against ownership records. Once approved, each person sees only their own property, charges, requests and community notices.

Residents & landlords
5

Daily services run

Maintenance requests move through review with resident-visible updates. Facility slots are requested and confirmed. Bills are issued, receipts recorded after a verified transfer, and expenses pass approval rules.

Everyone
6

Gate and safety

Assigned officers issue visitor passes, verify them at the gate, record entry and exit, report incidents and review the access history for their gate only.

Security officers

Who sees what

Access follows estate membership and verified relationships. A person with several roles gets the appropriate workspace for each sign-in.

Platform operations

Oversees all estates, reviews new applications, grants platform roles and manages the manual balance ledger with a full audit trail.

Estate team

Administrators, managers, finance staff and security officers work inside one estate: properties, people, money, facilities and gates.

Residents

See approved property access, tenancy terms, service charges, receipts, maintenance progress, notices and bookings for their own home.

Landlords

See verified ownership, occupancy, tenancy terms and maintenance requests linked only to the properties they own.

This is a working local presentation build

Connected workflows are backed by the Estate API and PostgreSQL. Payment-provider integration, outbound email/SMS delivery and account recovery are still being built. The demo walkthrough holds the local logins and gate pass code for presenters.
